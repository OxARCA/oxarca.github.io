Project *Transferable and Cost-Aware Source-Grounded AI for Archival Research*  has been
awarded a **British Academy/Leverhulme Small Research Grant** (SRG26\262244). The principal
investigators are David De Roure and Yishun Lu.

Our thanks to the British Academy and the Leverhulme Trust. Details of the scheme are
[here](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/funding/ba-leverhulme-small-research-grants/).
