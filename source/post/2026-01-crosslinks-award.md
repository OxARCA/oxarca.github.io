Our project — *Interdisciplinary Uses of AI in the Humanities: Developing an Epistemically
Grounded LLM-System for Archival Exploration* — has been funded by a **CrossLinks Award 2026**
from St Cross College, University of Oxford.

The question we are funded to answer is under which conditions AI systems can help historical scholarship. That work became [Archos](/page/archos.html).

Our thanks to St Cross College. For more information about the CrossLinks Award scheme, see [here](https://www.stx.ox.ac.uk/crosslinks) and the news [here](https://www.stx.ox.ac.uk/article/first-crosslinks-award-winners-announced).
